Our Story

A Legacy of Light & Lens
in the Heart of Mumbai

Established in the year 2000, D. Kumar Photo Studio has been a cornerstone of photography in Colaba, Mumbai. For over two decades, families, couples, and professionals have trusted us to capture their most cherished moments with artistry and care.

Our studio at Darshan Heritage, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road — opposite 4th Pasta Lane — is a warm, welcoming space where technical excellence meets genuine human connection. Every click of the shutter tells a story that will be treasured for generations.

With expertise spanning portrait, candid, wedding, maternity, newborn photography and videography, we bring a complete creative vision to every session — ensuring you leave with memories as beautiful as the moments themselves.
